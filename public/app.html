eRIGHT Door — the phone app

Signs you in when your phone joins the office Wi-Fi, out when it has left the Wi-Fi and is over 150 m from the office, and raises a full-screen alarm with an I'M SAFE button during a fire roll call. Android 10 or newer.

Set it up — about 2 minutes

  1. Install: open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow installs from this browser: Settings → Allow from this source, go back, Install.
  2. Link it to you: open your personal I'm SAFE link (the admin sends it to you) on this phone, on the office Wi-Fi, and tap 📱 Set up the eRIGHT Door app.
  3. Permissions (the app lists them with a Fix button each):
    • Notifications — Allow
    • Location — Precise, then Allow all the time (how the app knows you have left; your location stays on the phone)
    • Full-screen alarm — on
    • Battery — Unrestricted (Samsung otherwise puts it to sleep)
  4. Tap Test the alarm in the app: the roll-call screen should fill the phone. Nothing is sent.

Your location is never sent anywhere: the phone learns where the office is while it is on the office Wi-Fi and only ever tells the register "in" or "out". Uninstall it any time; your sign-ins at the door screen keep working as before.
