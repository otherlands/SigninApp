Devices & tokens

Give each card reader, scanner and wall button its own token with only what it needs. A device's token also fixes its name in the log, and one lost device can be revoked without re-keying the rest. The shared API_TOKEN keeps working until you remove it from the server.

Presence — phones & key tags

Belt and braces: a phone on the office Wi-Fi and a BLE tag on the keys. Either one seen inside signs a person in automatically. Nothing is ever signed out by inference unless the sign-in was itself inferred and both have gone and the tag walked out past a door; otherwise the register just shows 📵 probably left. A card or kiosk tap always wins.
